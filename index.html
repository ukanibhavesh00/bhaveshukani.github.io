How a project runs

  1. Scoping call. We agree who uses the report, the decisions it supports and alternatives that could save effort.
  2. Written requirements. Data sources, tables, keys, KPI definitions and any existing Excel reports are documented.
  3. Timeline agreed. Milestones and deadlines are confirmed before build starts.
  4. Model and build. Data is extracted, cleaned and modelled, then the report is built on a validated dataset.
  5. Review with you. You test the numbers against your own, and we refine layout and logic together.
  6. Handover. Documentation of measures and data flow, plus a walkthrough for your team.
