About Me

I’m a Power BI Developer / Data Analyst focused on building practical BI solutions from messy source data through to polished dashboards and automated reporting.

My work covers the full reporting lifecycle: requirement gathering, data extraction, transformation, data modelling, DAX, visual design, validation, stakeholder communication and production delivery.

My recent experience has been in maritime risk and operations analytics, working across Commercial, Operations, Intelligence, Support and Compliance reporting.

I enjoy solving the part of analytics that is often hidden behind the dashboard — defining the right metric, fixing data quality issues, designing a reliable model and making the final output genuinely useful.

02DELIVERY METHOD

Requirement Gathering & Delivery

A structured approach from business question to validated production report.

01

Discover

Meet users and stakeholders, understand the business question, pain points, decisions and expected output.

02

Define

Document requirements, data sources, tables, keys, calculations, filters, audiences and delivery expectations.

03

Build

Extract and transform data, resolve quality issues, build the model and develop measures and visuals.

04

Validate

Reconcile numbers, test business logic, run UAT and iterate with stakeholders before production.

05

Automate

Where useful, connect Power Automate, APIs or Microsoft business systems to reduce manual effort.

06

Handover

Present the final output, explain the logic, document the solution and support production.
